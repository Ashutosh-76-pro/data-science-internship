## 5. Capstone business problem

A retail manager needs to understand sales performance, profitability, returns, regional/category differences and monthly changes.

The analysis therefore asks:

1. What happened?
2. What changed?
3. Which region/category/segment performed strongly?
4. Which products need attention?
5. What data-quality issues could affect the conclusion?
6. What should management investigate next?

## 6. Key analytical outputs

The repository's completed capstone reports **₹450,930 Net Sales**, **₹138,890 Profit**, **30.80% Profit Margin** and **7.50% Return Rate** after the documented cleaning treatment.

It also identifies:

- March as the peak month.
- April as the month requiring investigation because Net Sales fell 13.5% versus March.
- Corporate as the largest segment by Net Sales.
- North as the leading region by Net Sales.
- Monitor as a high-revenue but comparatively low-margin product.

## 7. Important integrity note

The retail dataset is synthetic and intentionally designed for training. It must not be represented as confidential or real company data.

The analysis separates observations from explanations: a pattern in the dataset is not automatically proof of causation.
